Operational Capabilities

Understanding how the KibbleScan network maintains analytical vigilance.

Why shouldn't I just read the back of the bag?
By the time a new printed bag reaches your local shelf, the formulation may have already been changed in the supply chain for months. Furthermore, minor shifts in ingredient hierarchy (which dictate clinical volume ratios) are incredibly difficult to audit manually. The system automates this vigilance.
What exactly does "ingredient drift" mean?
Ingredient drift occurs when a manufacturer subtly alters a product's formulation—often substituting high-quality proteins for cheaper derivatives or elevating controversial preservatives—while keeping front-of-house marketing identical. It is a recognized industry practice designed to optimize manufacturer margins.
What triggers a Pro alert?
Pro Surveillance accounts receive instant data-push notifications (SMS) the moment our system detects any addition, removal, or volumetric re-ordering of ingredients in tracked profiles. Marketing communications are strictly prohibited; users only receive raw, actionable intelligence.
Are there long-term commitments?
No. Services operate on a month-to-month or annual basis. You can downgrade to Basic Analysis or terminate your Pro Surveillance subscription entirely from within your dashboard at any time.
